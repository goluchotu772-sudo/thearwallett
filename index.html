06 / SAFETY

Is Ar wallet Safe or Fake?

Many users consider Ar wallet safe. However, scammers may impersonate Ar wallet support to steal your OTP. Never share your OTP or password with anyone.

Signs of a Legitimate Wallet App

  • Fast transactions with high order availability
  • Clear terms shown before every transaction
  • No direct holding of user funds (C2C model)
  • Support only through official in app live chat

Warning Signs of a Scam

  • Anyone asking for OTP or password via phone or Telegram
  • Login links sent through unknown messages
  • Websites that look like Ar wallet but have different URLs
  • Anyone claiming to be Ar wallet support and contacting you first
!

Ar wallet never contacts users first. To test a suspicious site, enter a wrong password if it still logs in, it is a phishing site.

Legal Status in India

Ar wallet is not regulated by RBI and is considered outside India's legal financial framework. A few accounts have reportedly been frozen. Use it at your own risk and start with small amounts.

Can I create multiple accounts?
